Tutorial: make it yours

I asked that the first start ask which theme, display mode and sounds the player wants. These are three new steps in the tutorial, each with a live preview. A pick is saved at once to the same setting MENU uses, and each step says where to change it later. START: skip still works: a step not reached keeps its default (Light, Spotlight, Wood, Menu Music on).

Try them with the pad below (or the arrow keys and Enter): LEFT and RIGHT look at each choice, A keeps it. The Sounds step plays the launcher's own sounds. Pick one option in each section, and say what to change in the note.
