Side lights lab

The four lights under POWER can be lit one by one, so they can show more than the battery. Each section below plays an option on a drawing of the Pixel 2's right side, top to bottom as on the device. Press Play, pick one option in each section, and add a note if you want a change. The panel under this text lets you try every pattern together, with your picks.

Connecting…

Kept in every option
  • On or off, nothing between. The lights are GPIO pins with no brightness. The bottom light is red, green, or both at once, which the simulator draws amber; how amber it looks on the device is still to be checked.
  • The low-battery flash is never hidden unless the low-battery part is turned off. The red light shows through every new pattern.
  • L2+R2 still flashes the battery level over everything, in every setting.
  • No cost to games. The gauge sleeps until something happens; a pattern is a few wakes of a small program of its own. The launcher sends one short line to the gauge and never waits for it. Diatom does nothing new, so a game loses no frames.
  • Where: volume and brightness in menus and in games (ports too), the game light for every game the launcher starts, Done for File Transfer (USB and Wi-Fi) and the Scraper.
