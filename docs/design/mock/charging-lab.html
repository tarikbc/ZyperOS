Charging lab

A screen for when the Pixel 2 is off and charging: how full it is, how fast it charges and when it will be full. Today a charger wakes the handheld for a moment and the bootloader turns it straight off again, so the screen stays dark. Pick one option in each section, and add a note if you want a change. Every option is shown in Beard and in Salt & Pepper.

Connecting…

Kept in every option
  • When it shows: a charger goes in while the handheld is off. The handheld starts a small charging mode instead of turning off again. No game, no music, no Wi-Fi; the side lights show the charge, as they do when it is on.
  • The numbers come from the battery: the charge (%), the power going in (W, from the current and the voltage), and the kind of charger (a wall charger gets 2 A, a computer 1.5 A). "Full in" is worked out from the charge still needed and the current, smoothed over a minute, and slower for the last 10%, as a battery takes longer there. It shows after the first 30 seconds, rounded to 5 minutes.
  • Unplugged: the screen says "Unplugged" for a moment and the handheld turns off.
  • Needs a new bootloader once: the bootloader decides what a charger does to a handheld that is off. The change goes onto the card with the installer or a system update, after you say yes.
