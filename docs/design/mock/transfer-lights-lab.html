Transfer lights lab

While files move to or from the card, the four side lights can show it: in Flash Drive (the card as a USB drive) and on File Transfer's web page, over Wi-Fi or the cable. Each section plays an option on a drawing of the Pixel 2's right side, top to bottom as on the device. Press Play, pick one option in each section, and add a note if you want a change. The panel under this text lets you try it all together, with your picks.

Connecting…

Kept in every option
  • Download is data that comes in to the card (the computer writes, or the web page sends a file to the handheld). Upload is data that goes out of the card (the computer reads, or the page downloads a file). The same words and arrows as the Flash Drive screen's two tiles.
  • Only while bytes move. About 1 s after the last bytes, the lights show what they showed before: the charging bar on the cable, or nothing. Flash Drive looks once a second, as its tiles do. A way that moves less than 64 KB in that second counts as still (a computer's own small writes when it opens the drive, a folder list).
  • Green only. The low-battery red still shows through, and L2+R2 still flashes the battery level over everything.
  • Newest on top, as today. The transfer takes the lights over the charging bar. A volume or brightness change, a Done or a sparkle shows over the transfer for its time, then the transfer comes back while files still move.
  • Off is off. MENU > Side Lights decides, as for every part. A card with every part off shows nothing.
  • No cost to the transfer. Flash Drive sends one short line a second and the web page at most two; neither waits for the lights. The gauge wakes only for each step of the pattern. No game is involved: File Transfer closes before a game starts.
