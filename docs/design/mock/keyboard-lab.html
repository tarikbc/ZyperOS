ZyperOS Keyboard Lab

The whole on-screen keyboard, as src/keyboard.c lays it out: the digit row on every layer, three layers, the space bar, and the face buttons as verbs. Each option is shown in Beard on the letters and in Salt & Pepper on the symbols.

Connecting…

Kept in every option, because each saves presses on a D-pad: B deletes, X shifts, Y switches to symbols, so a capital costs one press; the digits never need a layer switch; L1/R1 move the cursor; the grid wraps at every edge; the text is never hidden. The selected key is the lime focus fill, and each press clicks and pops.
