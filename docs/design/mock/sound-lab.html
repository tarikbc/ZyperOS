ZyperOS Sound Lab

The six interface sounds DESIGN.md names, as the handheld will play them: each is a WAV that tools/gensounds.py synthesizes, at 48 kHz and at the handheld's own level. Play them on live screens, then pick a voice and three rules for when they play.

Connecting…

Kept in every option. Six sounds: move (a step), edge (a step that hits the end), accept (A, and a menu opening), back (B, a menu closing), deny (a press nothing can act on, such as Y on Muse's albums) and reward (a favorite added, a tutorial step). They are quiet and short, they come from the launcher only, and never over a game. The volume keys and the mute switch set them. They play on the speaker, the headphone jack and a USB-C DAC, and stay off on a Bluetooth headset, whose delay would put every tick after its press. MENU > UI Sounds turns them off.
Click a screen, then ← ↑ → ↓ move, hold to repeat, Enter is A, Backspace is B, Y is Y, M is MENU. Or use the buttons under it.
