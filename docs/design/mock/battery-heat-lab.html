Battery and heat lab

Two safety screens. First, what the handheld shows when it starts with a battery too low to play: today it boots to the home and powers off about 10 seconds later with no word. Second, a warning when the chip gets hot and starts to slow down: today nothing shows. Pick one option in each section, and add a note if you want a change. Every option is shown in Beard and in Salt & Pepper.

Connecting…

Kept in every option
  • Low battery at start: 2% or less, off the charger (the level where a game already saves and the handheld powers off). The screen comes before the home, the tutorial and a resume into a game, so nothing starts and stops again. It costs a normal start nothing: the launcher reads the battery for the status corner anyway. POWER powers off at once. The game you were in stays marked, so the next start, charged, goes back into it.
  • Heat: the warning comes when the chip passes 85 °C, where the kernel slows the CPU and GPU hard. The handheld reads the temperature every 5 seconds, two readings in a row, so one odd reading says nothing. The warning comes again only after the chip has cooled under 80 °C and 5 minutes have passed. No sound: the slow-down is not something you did.
  • At 115 °C the kernel powers the handheld off. That power-off already saves a running game, like POWER, and now also keeps the game marked so the next start goes back into it.
