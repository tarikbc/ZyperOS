ZyperOS Menu Music Lab

Twelve free 8-bit tracks for the menus of a fresh card, ten of them original and two covers of public-domain classics. Every one may ship in ZyperOS's public repository and on its card image. Listen, then pick 3 to 5.

Connecting…

Kept whatever you pick. These play only while Roms/BGM holds no tracks: a card's own music always wins, and takes over as soon as it is copied on. MENU > Menu Music shuffles them (each once before any plays again) or plays one over and over, at its own volume under the system's, and never over a game, a port or MP4. They ship as Ogg Vorbis at 48 kHz, the rate the handheld mixes at, all at the same loudness, under 15 MB together. Every one is CC0 or CC BY, and each is credited in THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md and the README.
