Charge limit lab

A setting that stops charging at 80, 85 or 90%, to keep the battery healthy for longer. Pick one option in each section, and add a note if you want a change. Every option is shown in Beard and in Salt & Pepper.

Connecting…

Kept in every option
  • How it works: at the limit the Pixel 2's charger stops, and it starts again when the battery falls 5% under the limit (for 85%: it stops at 85% and starts again at 80%). So it does not top up all day on a desk. The percentage stays true: 100% is still a full battery.
  • Off and charging too: a charger on a Pixel 2 that is off starts the charging screen, and the charging screen keeps the limit.
  • The time on the charging screen is to the limit: "85% in about 40 min", not "Full in about 1 h 10 min".
  • Why these numbers: a lithium battery ages slower when it is not kept full. 80 to 90% keeps most of a charge's play time and takes away the hardest part for the cell, the last 10%.
  • Needs plastron once: the kernel's charger driver for the Pixel 2 has no limit today. A small change adds the standard Linux one (charge_control_end_threshold), and it comes with a system update. The Brick's charger belongs to its own kernel, so the row shows on the Pixel 2 only for now.
