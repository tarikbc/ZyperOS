Test kit 2 of ZyperOS for the TrimUI Brick, for the testers in its thread on the [ZyperOS Discord](https://discord.gg/Ust8sE22Tm). It is not a release: the Brick joins a release once its kits pass, and until then neither System Update nor the installer of a release offers it. Thank you for trying it.

ZyperOS runs from its own microSD card and never writes to the Brick's own storage. Take the card out, and your Brick starts its own system, as before.

## You need

- A TrimUI Brick (TG3040) or Brick Hammer, charged to half or more. Not the Brick Pro yet.
- A spare microSD card, 8 GB or more. Everything on it is erased.
- A computer (macOS, Windows or Linux) with internet, and Wi-Fi for the Brick.
- One game of your own for the Game Boy Advance or the SNES.

## The test

Do these in order. For each number, write "ok" or what went wrong. A photo only where a step says so.

1. **Install.** Download ZyperOS Installer for your computer from this page's files below: `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.6-kit.2-macOS.zip`, `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.6-kit.2-Windows.exe` or `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.6-kit.2-amd64.deb`. This kit's installer offers this kit, and only on the Brick. The first time it opens:
   - **macOS:** unzip it and open the app. It isn't signed by Apple, so macOS stops it once: open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and choose **Open Anyway**. Writing the card asks for your password.
   - **Windows:** if Windows says it protected your PC, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It asks to run as administrator, which writing the card needs.
   - **Linux (Debian, Ubuntu, Mint):** `sudo apt install ./ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.6-kit.2-amd64.deb`, then open ZyperOS Installer from your apps. This kit has no Fedora package; a release has one.

   Pick **TrimUI Brick**, your spare card and **Fresh install**, and press **Install**. The Brick's graphics and Wi-Fi need closed files made for its chips (Imagination's GPU driver and XRadio's Wi-Fi firmware), which ZyperOS does not pass on. So the installer downloads them for you from where they are published, Creality's Sonic Pad SDK on GitHub, checks each one, and puts them only on your card. *Look for:* it ends with no error. *If not:* a screenshot of its message.
2. **First start.** Put the card in the Brick and turn it on. *Look for:* the ZyperOS start screen, then a short line under it that names your screen's panel, then the first-start setup. *If the screen stays black, or the picture is shifted, stretched or has wrong colors:* a photo of the screen, and stop here (send the logs as in step 12, from the card).
3. **The picture.** Go through the first-start setup. *Look for:* a sharp picture that fills the screen, the right way up, with no flicker or lines. *If not:* a photo.
4. **The tutorial.** It starts by itself after setup (later: **MENU > Show Tutorial**). Do every lesson. *Look for:* each key it asks for works, and the key that lights on the drawn Brick is the one you pressed. *If not:* the lesson's name and the key.
5. **Brightness and volume.** Press F1 and F2, then VOL+ and VOL-. *Look for:* F1 and F2 change the brightness; the volume keys change the volume, and the sound comes from both speakers. With headphones in, the sound moves to them.
6. **Wi-Fi.** **MENU > Wi-Fi**, and join your network. *Look for:* it connects, and **MENU > About** shows an address.
7. **Copy a game over Wi-Fi.** **MENU > Wi-Fi File Transfer**. Open the address it shows in a browser on your computer, and drop your game into its console's folder. *Look for:* the game shows on its shelf.
8. **Play.** Play the game for about five minutes. *Look for:* clean sound with no crackle, and every button works. You don't need to judge the speed: the logs record how fast each game ran.
9. **The in-game menu.** Press MENU during the game. Save a state, load it, open the **Controls** tab, then **Quit**. *Look for:* the state loads where you saved it, the Controls tab shows the Brick, and Quit goes back to the shelf.
10. **Sleep.** Press POWER once. Wait ten seconds and press it again. *Look for:* the screen goes off and comes back where you left it.
11. **Charging.** Plug in the charger while ZyperOS runs. *Look for:* the battery shows it charges.
12. **Send the logs.** Turn the Brick on. In the browser page of step 7, press **Download logs**. Post the file in this kit's Discord thread with your model (Brick or Brick Hammer), its color, and your list of 1 to 12. *If Wi-Fi didn't work:* turn the Brick off, put the card in your computer, and zip the folder `.userdata/brick/logs` instead.

That is all. The logs tell me your panel, buttons, GPU, Wi-Fi chip and how long each part of the start took, so you don't need to look those up.

## Good to know

- **Not in this kit:** System Update, Bluetooth, PortMaster's ports and the Brick's lights. The consoles whose cores draw with the GPU (Dreamcast, NAOMI, Atomiswave, Nintendo 64, PSP and Saturn) are hidden for now; they come once those cores run on the Brick's GPU.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games. BIOS files, where a console needs them, go in `Bios/`.
- **Going back:** take the card out, and the Brick starts its own system. To use the card for something else, format it on your computer.
