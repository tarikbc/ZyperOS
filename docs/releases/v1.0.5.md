ZyperOS 1.0.5 lets a friend play your NES games from their phone, keeps your folders and your EmulationStation library as they are, puts MENU in tabs with a theme of your own, and adds the PSP, Doom, ColecoVision and TIC-80.

## Install

1. Download **ZyperOS Installer** for your computer below: macOS (`.zip`), Windows (`.exe`), or Linux (`.deb` or `.rpm`).
2. Put the microSD card in the computer, open the installer, and pick the GKD Pixel 2, the latest release and the card. **Fresh install** erases the card and puts ZyperOS on it.
3. Put the card in the Pixel 2 and turn it on. The first start sets the card up (about a minute), then asks for your language, and Zyper shows you each button.

Or write `ZyperOS-v1.0.5-pixel2.img.xz` with any disk image writer (Raspberry Pi Imager, balenaEtcher). Check it against `SHA256SUMS` first.

## What's new

- **Player 2 on a friend's phone:** in an NES game, **MENU > Player 2** lets a friend play the second controller in a browser, with no app and no account. With a Wi-Fi adapter in, the handheld makes a network just for the game and shows its QR code: the friend scans it, and the game opens on the phone with a touch pad under the picture. A computer on the USB cable opens `zyperos.local/play` and plays with the keyboard. The page speaks the handheld's language and wears its theme.
- **Your folders, kept:** folders inside a system's folder on the card are folders on its shelf, as deep as you like: "Homebrew", "Rom Hacks", "Translations". The folders come first, each a pixel-art folder in the system's color with how many games it holds. A opens one, B goes back up. A folder that holds one game's discs is still that one game. On File Transfer's page, drag games onto a folder to move them.
- **Covers or a list:** **START** on a system's shelf, or **MENU > View**, turns its row of covers into a list of names, with the focused game's cover and video snap beside it, and **START** again brings the covers back. Each system keeps its own.
- **Your EmulationStation library, as it is:** copy the roms folders from Knulli, ROCKNIX, ArkOS or ROGUE OS over as they are. Folders like `nds`, `psx` and `megadrive` are read as their systems, and the covers, video snaps, names and details their scrapers left are used.
- **Rename a game:** X on a game, then **Rename**, and type the new name. Its saves, save states, cover, video snap, achievements, favorite and play time follow it.
- **MENU in tabs:** Games, Display, Sound, Connections, System and About, with **L1** and **R1** going round them, across the whole top of the menu. Every list goes round too: Down on the last row goes to the first.
- **Your look:** **MENU > Display > Theme** picks the accent, a background tone for the light theme and one for the dark, rounded or square corners, and whether Zyper shows. **Auto Hours** sets when Auto turns light and dark.
- **Four new systems:** PSP (PPSSPP), Doom (prBoom), ColecoVision (Gearcoleco) and TIC-80. The PSP needs no BIOS, draws every frame, and has four of Deadstation Portable's ARM64 speedups. Doom plays WADs, id Software's own or the free Freedoom ones. ColecoVision needs `coleco.rom` in `Bios/`.
- **Shaders:** the **Shader** row in the in-game menu draws the game with Sharp-Shimmerless, zadpos's shader, whose pixels stay the same size so a scroll stays steady; as an old TV, with scanlines and a tube's colored stripes, flat or curved; with scanlines alone; or with a handheld's LCD grid. Set it per system, or for every system in **MENU > Display > Shader**. It is off to start with.
- **A screensaver of your games:** after a minute with nothing pressed on the shelf, the video snaps of random games play over the whole screen, each with its name. **START** starts the game on screen, and any other button goes back. Set it in **MENU > Display > Screensaver**.
- **Side lights while files move:** a wave runs along the side lights while files move to or from the card, in Flash Drive and on File Transfer's page, faster for a faster transfer. In Flash Drive they follow your brightness, and **MENU > Side Lights** plays each one on the lights as you move over it.
- **File Transfer, easier to join:** until a computer signs in, its screen shows the address and the PIN at full size, and over the cable a loading bar shows the computer connecting. Switches say On and Off.
- **Auto Off waits for long jobs:** scraping box art and videos, System Update's download, PortMaster's install, Storage and File Transfer's copies no longer get cut off by Auto Off.
- **Achievements offline:** an achievement you earn without Wi-Fi pops up at once as before, waits on the card, and goes to your RetroAchievements account, dated when you earned it, as soon as the handheld is on Wi-Fi again. The game's list says how many wait.
- **PortMaster that won't start:** PortMaster now starts again after a settings file of its own was cut off (the handheld turned off while it wrote), says what went wrong when it closes with an error, and has **Reinstall PortMaster** in its details, which puts a fresh copy back and keeps your ports.
- **Phones on the cable:** **What did you plug in?** has a third answer, **Phone**. It powers the phone and says how to make the phone the computer: on Android, tap the phone's USB notification, then **USB controlled by: This device**; on an iPhone, use a USB-C to USB-A adapter on the iPhone. Then Flash Drive and File Transfer work with the phone.
- **The colors as the game draws them:** every game and the shelf were shown a little darker and cooler than their own colors, from two color corrections made by eye. Now the screen shows what the game draws, as the stock firmware does.
- **A graphics driver crash fixed:** a bug in the graphics driver (Mesa) could crash a game or slow the picture down each time it reloaded the screen. PSP games such as LocoRoco hit it most. The system image has the fix.
- **Rumble that stops:** a Nintendo 64 game that stops its Rumble Pak now always stops the motor, where before the handheld could keep shaking.

## Updating from 1.0.4

**MENU > System Update** offers 1.0.5 over Wi-Fi, or use **Update** in ZyperOS Installer, which keeps your games, music and saves. From 1.0.3, File Transfer over the cable doesn't connect, so update over Wi-Fi or with the installer.

## Good to know

- **The GKD Pixel 2 only.** The TrimUI Brick's image comes in a later release.
- **Not included, add them if you want:** BIOS files, which go in `Bios/`. The README lists each one.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games.
