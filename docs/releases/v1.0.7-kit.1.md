Test kit 1.0.7-kit.1 of ZyperOS for the TrimUI Brick, for the testers in its thread on the [ZyperOS Discord](https://discord.gg/Ust8sE22Tm). It is not a release: the Brick joins a release once its kits pass, and until then neither System Update nor the installer of a release offers it. This kit brings most of what the Pixel 2 has to the Brick. Thank you for trying it.

ZyperOS runs from its own microSD card and never writes to the Brick's own storage. Take the card out, and your Brick starts its own system, as before.

## You need

- A TrimUI Brick (TG3040) or Brick Hammer, charged to half or more. Not the Brick Pro yet.
- A spare microSD card, 8 GB or more. Everything on it is erased, so use a spare card or copy your saves first.
- A computer (macOS, Windows or Linux) with internet, Wi-Fi for the Brick, and a USB charger.
- A Game Boy Advance or SNES game of your own. If you have them: a PSP, Nintendo 64, Dreamcast or Saturn game, and Bluetooth headphones or a pad.

## The test

Do these in order. For each number, write "ok" or what went wrong. A photo only where a step says so.

1. **Install.** Download ZyperOS Installer for your computer from this page's files below: `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.7-kit.1-macOS.zip`, `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.7-kit.1-Windows.exe` or `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.7-kit.1-amd64.deb`. This kit's installer offers this kit, and only on the Brick. The first time it opens:
   - **macOS:** unzip it and open the app. It isn't signed by Apple, so macOS stops it once: open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and choose **Open Anyway**. Writing the card asks for your password.
   - **Windows:** if Windows says it protected your PC, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It asks to run as administrator, which writing the card needs.
   - **Linux (Debian, Ubuntu, Mint):** `sudo apt install ./ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.7-kit.1-amd64.deb`, then open ZyperOS Installer from your apps. This kit has no Fedora package; a release has one.

   Pick **TrimUI Brick**, your spare card and **Fresh install**, and press **Install**. The Brick's graphics and Wi-Fi need closed files made for its chips (Imagination's GPU driver and XRadio's Wi-Fi firmware), which ZyperOS does not pass on. So the installer downloads them for you from where they are published, Creality's Sonic Pad SDK on GitHub, checks each one, and puts them only on your card. *Look for:* it ends with no error. *If not:* a screenshot of its message.
2. **Screen Colors.** **MENU > Display > Screen Colors.** Move Temperature to +10, then Saturation to +10 and -10, then go back to Standard. *Look for:* the whole screen changes as you move each one. A photo of the Screen Colors page at Temperature +10.
3. **Night Mode.** **MENU > Display > Night Mode:** On, Night Warmth 8. *Look for:* the whole screen turns warm at once, the games too. Then turn it Off.
4. **The old screens.** Copy your GBA or SNES game over Wi-Fi as in kit 2 (**MENU > Wi-Fi File Transfer**), and start it. In the in-game menu, **Display > Shader:** try CRT, CRT Curved, Scanlines, LCD Grid and LCD No Backlight, and Strength at 50%. A photo of one. *Look for:* the game still runs at full speed.
5. **On-Screen Stats.** In the same game, turn On-Screen Stats on. A photo.
6. **The home's own Shader.** **MENU > Display > Shader:** CRT Curved on the home. A photo. Then turn it Off.
7. **The GPU consoles.** A PSP, Nintendo 64, Dreamcast or Saturn game, whichever you have. *Look for:* it starts, and it plays at full speed for a minute.
8. **A charger.** Plug the Brick into a USB charger. *Look for:* no question comes, and it charges.
9. **A computer.** Plug the Brick into a computer. *Look for:* "Computer Connected" asks once. Try **Charge Only** (nothing appears on the computer), then unplug and plug again (it asks again), **File Transfer** (the page opens on the computer), and **Flash Drive** (the card shows as a drive: copy a file to it, eject it on the computer, and the Brick comes back).
10. **Started on the cable.** Turn the Brick off, plug it into the computer, and turn it on. *Look for:* the question comes once the home is up.
11. **Bluetooth.** Pair headphones or a pad (**MENU > Connections > Bluetooth**). *Look for:* a game plays through them.
12. **The rumble motor.** **MENU > About**, then press **Y**: the Hardware Check opens. On **Rumble motor**, press A once. *Look for:* the Brick buzzes, for about a third of a second.
13. **The lights.** On **Lights**, press A. Each light comes on alone for a second, one after another (about 25 seconds in all; the screen waits). Which lit, and in which order (the top bar, the shoulder keys, F1 and F2, the light by POWER)? A short video is the easiest answer.
14. **Send the logs.** In the browser page of File Transfer, press **Download logs**. Post the file in this kit's Discord thread with your model (Brick or Brick Hammer), its color, and your list of 1 to 14. *If Wi-Fi didn't work:* turn the Brick off, put the card in your computer, and zip the folder `.userdata/brick/logs` instead.

That is all. The logs tell me your panel, buttons, GPU, lights, the motor's power and how fast each game ran, so you don't need to look those up.

## Good to know

- **Not in this kit:** System Update, PortMaster's ports, and rumble in games: the Hardware Check's rumble test comes first, and games get it once a kit passes it.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games. BIOS files, where a console needs them, go in `Bios/`.
- **Going back:** take the card out, and the Brick starts its own system. To use the card for something else, format it on your computer.
