ZyperOS 1.0.1 brings eight languages, Nintendo DS at full speed, and a lot of small things I wanted after living with 1.0 on my own Pixel 2.

## Install

1. Download **ZyperOS Installer** for your computer below: macOS (`.zip`), Windows (`.exe`), or Linux (`.deb` or `.rpm`).
2. Put the microSD card in the computer, open the installer, and pick the GKD Pixel 2, the latest release and the card. **Fresh install** erases the card and puts ZyperOS on it.
3. Put the card in the Pixel 2 and turn it on. The first start sets the card up (about a minute), then asks for your language, and Zyper shows you each button.

Or write `ZyperOS-v1.0.1-pixel2.img.xz` with any disk image writer (Raspberry Pi Imager, balenaEtcher). Check it against `SHA256SUMS` first.

## What's new

- **Eight languages:** English, 简体中文, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, 日本語 and Italiano. Every screen follows the language you pick, the in-game menu, the charging screen, the File Transfer page and the line under the boot animation included. Change it any time in **MENU > Language**.
- **Nintendo DS at full speed:** DSperate now runs its threads the way it was designed to, at the CPU's top clock. A Mario Kart DS race on my Pixel 2 went from 49 to about 60 fps.
- **Delete a game** from its details page (X on the shelf). It asks first, then removes the game with its saves, states, cover and video. Shared files and BIOS sets stay.
- **Favorites** shows each game's system right under its name.
- **Video snaps** play the moment the bar under the cover is full.
- **Faster home navigation:** Up and Down on the home jump 3 systems in release-date order, or to the next letter when the systems go A to Z.
- **Menus stop at their ends** instead of wrapping around, and the story in **About** stays still until you scroll it with Up and Down.
- **Charge Limit** holds the battery within 2 points of the limit (80% stays between 78 and 80), instead of letting it fall 5 points first.
- **The first start's tour** reacts to L2 and R2 when they're not the button it asks for.
- **Smoother covers on the shelf** and fixes to the File Transfer page.

## Updating from 1.0.0

**MENU > System Update** finds 1.0.1 over Wi-Fi, or use **Update** in ZyperOS Installer, which keeps your games, music and saves.

## Good to know

- **The GKD Pixel 2 only.** The TrimUI Brick's image comes in a later release.
- **Not included, add them if you want:** BIOS files, which go in `Bios/`, and DraStic, which goes in `Emulators/DraStic/`. The README lists each one.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games.
