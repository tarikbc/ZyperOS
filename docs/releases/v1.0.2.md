ZyperOS 1.0.2 brings PortMaster, a Storage page, smooth Nintendo 64, a new way to pick covers and videos, and many things I wanted after playing 1.0.1 every day.

## Install

1. Download **ZyperOS Installer** for your computer below: macOS (`.zip`), Windows (`.exe`), or Linux (`.deb` or `.rpm`).
2. Put the microSD card in the computer, open the installer, and pick the GKD Pixel 2, the latest release and the card. **Fresh install** erases the card and puts ZyperOS on it.
3. Put the card in the Pixel 2 and turn it on. The first start sets the card up (about a minute), then asks for your language, and Zyper shows you each button.

Or write `ZyperOS-v1.0.2-pixel2.img.xz` with any disk image writer (Raspberry Pi Imager, balenaEtcher). Check it against `SHA256SUMS` first.

## What's new

- **PortMaster:** install it from the Ports shelf, then pick ports from its own list. **MENU** over any port, PortMaster, OpenBOR or another program opens the in-game menu, with **Continue**, a real **Controls** page from the port's own files, and **Quit**. PortMaster has its own art in both themes.
- **MENU > Storage** shows how the card is used, as a phone does. Open a group to see what takes the most room, sort it with **X**, and delete what you don't want.
- **Smooth Nintendo 64:** ParallelN64 with Glide64 is the default now, as in ROGUE OS. Each button is the N64 button it names, the d-pad is the stick, the C buttons are **SELECT** with X, A, B or Y, and you choose the Rumble Pak or the Controller Pak in the in-game menu.
- **Cover and Video:** on a game's details (X), pick its cover from libretro, ScreenScraper and SteamGridDB, see each one big next to the one you have, and put the old one back if you change your mind. Download, watch, replace or delete its video snap there too.
- **Continue or start over:** **A** continues a game where you left it, and **Y** starts it from the start. Favorite moved to the game's details, and a heart before its name marks a favorite on its console's shelf.
- **Hotkeys in a game:** hold **SELECT** and press **R1** to save a state or **L1** to load it, into a **Quick Slot** you choose for each system. **SELECT + R2** and **SELECT + L2** play faster and slower.
- **X quits** from any row of the in-game menu, and **Controls** shows each system's own buttons, the PlayStation's shapes included.
- **MENU + START, held a second, takes you home** from anything: a game, a port or a screen that stopped answering. After a crash, the Pixel 2 now starts on the home instead of going back into the game.
- **MP4:** albums, books and films on one shelf, each with a small mark for its kind. A long song name slides in the top bar, Now Playing shows its buttons, and **X** or **SELECT + START** locks the screen, which wakes at its lowest brightness. Audio Channels **Auto** plays mono on the speaker and stereo on headphones.
- **Battery Time Left:** the battery icon can show the time left instead of the percentage, and says **On hold** at the charge limit and **Charged** when full.
- **Nintendo DS:** a **Game Language** row picks each game's language, and DSperate is the only DS emulator, so DraStic is no longer needed.
- **Achievements** have a clearer name and badge, and Zyper cheers each unlock.
- **Developer Mode:** hold **L2 + R2** on **About** for five seconds to turn on SSH over the USB cable.
- **Fixes:** text fits its box in all eight languages, the in-game clock no longer meets the battery, the play time line sits closer to the title, and a short video snap (a DS or Game Boy one) no longer restarts the launcher.

## Updating from 1.0.1

**MENU > System Update** offers **System and ZyperOS** over Wi-Fi or the USB cable: this release updates the system too, for PortMaster. Or use **Update** in ZyperOS Installer, which keeps your games, music and saves.

## Good to know

- **The GKD Pixel 2 only.** The TrimUI Brick's image comes in a later release.
- **Not included, add them if you want:** BIOS files, which go in `Bios/`. The README lists each one.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games.
