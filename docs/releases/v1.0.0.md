ZyperOS 1.0 is the first release: a fast, light firmware for the GKD Pixel 2 that plays your whole retro library. It is named after Zyper, my dog, who was quick, light on his feet and full of energy. [The story is in the README](https://github.com/tarikbc/ZyperOS#the-name).

## Install

1. Download **ZyperOS Installer** for your computer below: macOS (`.zip`), Windows (`.exe`), or Linux (`.deb` or `.rpm`).
2. Put the microSD card in the computer, open the installer, and pick the GKD Pixel 2, the latest release and the card. **Fresh install** erases the card and puts ZyperOS on it.
3. Put the card in the Pixel 2 and turn it on. The first start sets the card up (about a minute), then Zyper shows you each button and asks for the theme, the home screen and the music.

Or write `ZyperOS-v1.0.0-pixel2.img.xz` with any disk image writer (Raspberry Pi Imager, balenaEtcher). Check it against `SHA256SUMS` first.

Then add your games: put the card back in the computer, or use **MENU > Wi-Fi File Transfer** (or USB File Transfer over the cable) from any browser.

## What's in 1.0

- **Forty systems,** from the Atari 2600 to PlayStation, Nintendo 64, Nintendo DS, Dreamcast and Saturn, with Arcade, MAME, Neo Geo, NAOMI and Atomiswave, ScummVM, PICO-8, OpenBOR and J2ME, and a Ports shelf for PortMaster games.
- **A home screen in pixel art:** every system as its animated console, A to Z or by release date, on a Spotlight or a Gallery home, in light or dark or by the clock. Your games are a row of covers, and a cover turns into a few seconds of video when you rest on it.
- **Fast:** it starts in about two seconds, the shelf comes up about half a second after the launcher starts, and an idle home uses about 5% of one core.
- **Box art and details the device finds itself,** from libretro, ScreenScraper and SteamGridDB, with renamed ROM packs matched by checksum.
- **RetroAchievements** on the device, working offline once a game's list is in.
- **Wi-Fi and USB File Transfer** from any browser, behind a PIN, with the handheld's own network when there is no Wi-Fi around.
- **System Update over Wi-Fi:** signed, only the files that changed, into a second system slot that rolls back by itself if a new version does not start.
- **In every game:** autosave and resume, six save slots, fast forward (MENU + R1), turbo buttons, rumble, and a **Controls** page that shows what each button does in that game.
- **MP4,** a player for your music, audiobooks and videos, on SELECT from anywhere.
- **Menu music, side lights, battery care** (a charge limit, a charging screen while off, a heat warning), **Play Time**, **Favorites** and **Search Games**.

The full list, the systems and the controls are in the [README](https://github.com/tarikbc/ZyperOS#readme) and on [zyperos.com](https://zyperos.com).

## Good to know

- **The GKD Pixel 2 only.** The TrimUI Brick's image comes in a later release.
- **Updates from here on are over Wi-Fi** in **MENU > System Update**. A card that ran a development build before 1.0 gets them after one **Update** with ZyperOS Installer, which keeps its games and saves (a card from before the two system slots needs a fresh install).
- **Not included, add them if you want:** BIOS files, which go in `Bios/` (some systems need theirs), and DraStic, which goes in `Emulators/DraStic/`. The README lists each one.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games.
