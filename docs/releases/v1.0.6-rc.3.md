The third release candidate of ZyperOS 1.0.6 for the TrimUI Brick, for the testers in its thread on the [ZyperOS Discord](https://discord.gg/Ust8sE22Tm). It is the code 1.0.6 will ship, with every fix since rc.2 and a version of its own, and it is not a release: neither System Update nor the installer of a release offers it. 1.0.6 comes out for the GKD Pixel 2 first, and if rc.3 passes on a real Brick, the Brick's image joins it. Thank you for trying it.

ZyperOS runs from its own microSD card and never writes to the Brick's own storage. Take the card out, and your Brick starts its own system, as before.

## Since rc.2

- **File Transfer:** a console's own folder (GBA, NES and the rest, in ROMs and Save States) can no longer be moved, renamed or deleted from the page, so ZyperOS always finds its games there. Everything inside it still moves, renames and deletes as before.
- **Folder Color:** **MENU > Display > Theme** has a **Folder Color** row. **System Colors**, where it starts, keeps each folder in its system's color; or pick one of the ten accents for every folder, on the shelves and on File Transfer's page.
- **Grid:** a row of cards no longer loses its pictures for a frame while the grid scrolls, and games with no art glide as smoothly as covers.
- **Charging screen:** hold POWER for one second to start, not two, as for Power Button on Hold.
- **Achievements:** when a game has no list, the tab's sentence runs across the whole panel.
- **MSX2:** a second MSX2 game no longer crashes the emulator. Thanks to sylverb for the fix.

The fixes of rc.2 are on [its page](https://github.com/tarikbc/ZyperOS/releases/tag/v1.0.6-rc.2).

## You need

- A TrimUI Brick (TG3040) or Brick Hammer, charged to half or more. Not the Brick Pro yet.
- A microSD card, 8 GB or more: a test kit's or an earlier release candidate's card is fine. Everything on it is erased.
- A computer (macOS, Windows or Linux) with internet, and Wi-Fi for the Brick.
- One game of your own for the Game Boy Advance.

## The test

Do these in order. For each number, write "ok" or what went wrong. A photo only where a step says so.

1. **Install.** Download ZyperOS Installer from this page's files below: `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.6-rc.3-macOS.zip`, `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.6-rc.3-Windows.exe` or `ZyperOS-Installer-1.0.6-rc.3-amd64.deb`. It opens as the kit's did (on macOS, **Open Anyway** in Privacy & Security; on Windows, **More info** and **Run anyway**). Pick **TrimUI Brick**, the card and **Fresh install**, and press **Erase and install**. It downloads the Brick's closed files again, as the kit's did. *Look for:* it ends with no error. *If not:* a screenshot of its message.
2. **First start.** Put the card in the Brick and turn it on. *Look for:* the start screen, the line that names your screen's panel, the first-start setup and the tutorial. *If the screen stays black or looks wrong:* a photo, and stop here (send the logs as in step 9, from the card).
3. **The grid.** Copy your game over Wi-Fi as in the kit (**MENU > Wi-Fi File Transfer**). On its shelf, press **START** until the games show as **Grid**. On the home, press **START** until the systems show as **Grid**. *Look for:* covers in a grid on the shelf and systems in a grid on the home, and paging slides whole rows.
4. **The home's systems.** **MENU > Display > Home Systems**: turn the Game Boy Advance off, back out, then turn it on again. *Look for:* the system leaves the home and comes back, with its game. **Recents** shows the games you played.
5. **Play.** Play the game for about five minutes. In the shelf's menu, **Timing** shows **Smooth**; press **A** on it. *Look for:* Smooth and Exact side by side, then even motion, clean sound and every button working in the game.
6. **The in-game menu.** Press MENU during the game. In the Display tab, turn **On-Screen Stats** on: *look for* the line over the game. In the Game tab, save a state, load it, then **Undo Load**: *look for* the game back where it was before the load. Then **Quit**.
7. **The Power Button.** **MENU > System > Power Button > Hold**. Hold POWER for one second. *Look for:* a bar that fills, then the Brick saves and turns off. Turn it on again.
8. **Sleep and charging.** Press POWER once, wait ten seconds, press it again: *look for* the screen coming back where you left it. Plug in the charger: *look for* the battery showing it charges.
9. **Send the logs.** In the File Transfer page in your browser, press **Download logs**. Post the file in the release candidate's Discord thread with your model (Brick or Brick Hammer), its color, and your list of 1 to 9. *If Wi-Fi didn't work:* turn the Brick off, put the card in your computer, and zip the folder `.userdata/brick/logs` instead.

That is all.

## Good to know

- **From rc.1 or rc.2:** install rc.3 fresh, as in step 1. System Update never offers a release candidate.
- **Not on the Brick yet:** the consoles whose cores draw with the GPU (Dreamcast, NAOMI, Atomiswave, Nintendo 64, PSP and Saturn) are hidden; they come once those cores run on the Brick's GPU. PortMaster's ports, Flash Drive, Screen Colors, Night Mode, the old-screen shaders (CRT, CRT Curved, Scanlines, LCD Grid and LCD No Backlight; Sharp-Shimmerless is there), NES Player 2, the charge limit and the Brick's lights are not there yet either, and Bluetooth is not tested on it.
- **When the Brick's image is in 1.0.6,** **MENU > System Update** on this card offers it. Until then, it finds nothing for the Brick.
- **Keep this installer for now:** once 1.0.6 is out for the Pixel 2, this installer offers **Update to 1.0.6** for itself. Leave it until the Brick's image is in 1.0.6: until then, 1.0.6's installer has nothing for the Brick.
- **Your own games only.** ZyperOS comes with no games. BIOS files, where a console needs them, go in `Bios/`.
- **Going back:** take the card out, and the Brick starts its own system. To use the card for something else, format it on your computer.
