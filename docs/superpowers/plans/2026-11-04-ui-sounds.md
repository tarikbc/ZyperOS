# UI Sounds Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Phase 5 of the design-system roadmap: the six DESIGN.md 9 sounds, played by the launcher on the speaker, the headphone jack and a USB-C DAC, never on Bluetooth and never over a game, with MENU > UI Sounds to pick the voice or turn them off.

**Architecture:** The sounds are WAVs, `res/sounds/<voice>/<sound>.wav`, synthesized by `tools/gensounds.py` from the tones in `tools/designdef.py` (no recorded audio). `src/uisound.c` is the rule and the arithmetic with no SDL, ALSA or threads: which press plays what, where it may be heard, the setting, the WAV reader and the mix. `src/uisound_out.c` is the player: one SDL thread that reads every voice, opens ALSA by name (`default`, the system's dmix in front of the codec, or the DAC's `usb`) when there is a sound, mixes 10 ms slices no more than 30 ms ahead of what is heard, drains and lets go after 10 s of quiet or at once before a game; SDL's audio on the desktop build. The main thread only appends a voice under a lock. Hooks in `src/main.c` and `src/keyboard.c` are one call each where a press is decided.

**Tech Stack:** C (SDL2 threads, libasound), Python 3 standard library for the generator, the existing `make check` suite.

**Spec:** `DESIGN.md` 9 (sound), 11.5 (keyboard), 11.10 (tutorial reward); `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-design-system-roadmap.md` (phase 5); the sound lab, `docs/design/mock/sound-lab.html` (https://claude.ai/artifact/XAZUkvVyV2otmpQyjF8nRf).

## Decided in the sound lab (2026-11-04)

- **Voice:** Wood (soft mallets on wooden bars) is the default, and the voice is a setting: MENU > UI Sounds steps Wood, Glass, Bubble, Off. Glass is what the design lab played; Bubble is drops that pop.
- **A held direction:** the first step at full level, every repeat at half (`DS_SOUND_HELD_LEVEL`).
- **The step that wraps a shelf round:** plays move, with the normal held attenuation.
- **The row:** under Audio Channels.

## Global Constraints

- Every tone is typed once, in `tools/designdef.py`; `make sounds` writes the WAVs, `make design` the tables and `src/design.h`. `check-sounds` and `check-design` fail when they disagree.
- A press never waits on the sound: no file read, open or write on the main thread.
- Bluetooth is silent: `uisound_device` is NULL exactly when `aout_resolve` is `AOUT_BT`.
- No mixer control is touched: the volume keys, the mute switch and the DAC's software volume sit below the mix.
- The edge at the end of a list plays once for a press: a hold against the end is silent after it.

## Review Focus

1. A held direction on the shelf (11 steps a second) and against the end of a menu: one edge, then quiet.
2. The first sound after 10 s of quiet: the output opens then, so it may be a few ms late, and the codec may pop as it wakes.
3. A game launched, and the in-game menu's Continue: the accept and the back end whole, and nothing plays over the game.
4. Muse playing, with menus and the shelf moving: the sounds mix with the music, no gap or click in it.
5. A Bluetooth headset connected: silence, and the row still names the voice.
