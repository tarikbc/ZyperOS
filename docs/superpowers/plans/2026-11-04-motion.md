# Motion Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Phase 4 of the design-system roadmap: every motion in the launcher uses DESIGN.md 8's curves and durations, menus open and close one panel at a time, one-line messages fade and hold for one of two tokens, and the keyboard's keys pop when pressed.

**Architecture:** The tokens already exist in `src/design.h` (`DS_MS_*`, `ds_out`, `ds_in`, `ds_turn`); only the volume bar uses them today. Coverflow, the launch zoom and the power-off move onto them. A small menu-motion module draws a menu's panel through a render target, scaled and faded, and the menu loops (`menu_run` and the screens that loop on their own) ask it where the panel is in its open or close; the scrim follows the outermost menu. Messages get a short loop of their own instead of a frame and a sleep. Every motion is wall-clock driven, and shots stay at rest unless a shot asks for a time.

**Tech Stack:** C (SDL2), the existing `make check` suite and `--shot` harness.

**Spec:** `DESIGN.md` 8 (motion), 11.3 (menus), 11.5 (keyboard), 11.8 (one-line messages), 11.9 (pressed glyph at 92%), 14; the mock kit's motion (`docs/design/mock/components.html` 81-82, `kit.js` `ZK.animate`, `tokens.js` curves). Research notes (line numbers at 6b82d58, stale: find sites by content): `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-04-motion/research.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Durations and curves are tokens: `DS_MS_PRESS` 80, `QUICK` 120, `MOVE` 160, `EXIT` 140, `TURN` 300, `LAUNCH` 190, `FADE_OUT` 200, `HOLD_SHORT` 1600, `HOLD_LONG` 2200; `ds_out` (quintic) for things arriving, `ds_in` (cubic) for things leaving, `ds_turn` (smoothstep) for turns. No millisecond literal or hand-written easing for a motion DESIGN.md 8 names.
- One panel at a time: a submenu replaces its parent (the parent leaves, then the child arrives), never two panels on screen.
- Motion is wall-clock driven; a slow frame never slows it, and input is never delayed by it (a press during an animation acts at once).
- Shots stay at rest unless a shot asks for a time; `make check`'s shot comparisons do not change.
- While Diatom owns the display, the launcher does not present (the in-game menu finishes its exit before RESUME).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` has no device equivalent; none is added.

## Review Focus

1. A press during a menu's open or close: it acts at once, and the panel jumps to where it belongs.
2. MENU pressed deep in a submenu: every level unwinds and only the outermost panel animates out once.
3. The in-game menu: its exit finishes before RESUME, and nothing is presented while Diatom owns the display.
4. Held d-pad repeats every 90 ms in a menu and on the keyboard: the focus plate and the pop never lag or stack.
5. A message over a game or over the shelf: it fades in, holds its token, fades out, and B can dismiss it early.
