[about.story]
note = About, page 3, beside a photo of Zyper: Tarik's words about his dog Zyper, who died, after whom ZyperOS is named. Warm and plain; review: Tarik's own words, pt-BR above all. "zippy" and "hyper" are the two English words in the name: keep them in English and say what they mean. Signed "Tarik".
en = ZyperOS is named after Zyper, my dog, who passed away. He was quick, light on his feet and full of energy, and that is what this system tries to be.\n\nThe name is also two words in one: zippy and hyper. Games start the moment you press A, and the menus never make you wait.\n\nTarik
zh-Hans = ZyperOS 以我已经离开的狗狗 Zyper 命名。它敏捷、轻快、精力十足，这正是这个系统想成为的样子。\n\n这个名字也是两个英文单词的合体：zippy（敏捷）和 hyper（活力十足）。按下 A，游戏立刻开始；菜单也从不让你等待。\n\nTarik
es = ZyperOS lleva el nombre de Zyper, mi perro, que ya no está. Los juegos empiezan en cuanto pulsas A, y los menús nunca te hacen esperar.\n\nTarik
pt-BR = O ZyperOS leva o nome do Zyper, meu cachorro, que já se foi. Os jogos começam no instante em que você aperta A, e os menus nunca fazem você esperar.\n\nTarik

de = ZyperOS ist nach Zyper benannt, meinem Hund, der nicht mehr bei uns ist.
