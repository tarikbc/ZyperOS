# The tutorial (src/tour.c, src/tourdraw.c): Zyper's welcome, a step a
# button, the four choices and the finish. A step's title is at title size
# under the stage, its line and note at meta size under it, centered; keep
# them about as long as English's. Zyper's bubble may have "!"; nothing else
# does. Button names (A, MENU, VOL+, F1) are as printed, never translated;
# LEFT and RIGHT are the d-pad's, and are.
#
# tour.lang.* is the language step's, which comes later.
