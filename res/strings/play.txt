# NES player 2's page (res/web/play, served by p2d): what a friend's phone
# or laptop shows while it is player 2 of the game on the handheld, in the
# handheld's language. tools/i18n.py writes these into res/web/i18n/<code>.js
# beside the File Transfer page's, by their whole ID; play.js's t() reads
# them. The page's style is the File Transfer page's (web.txt): sentence
# case, messages end with a full stop. The pad's buttons keep the names
# printed on the handheld (A, B, SELECT, START), never translated.
