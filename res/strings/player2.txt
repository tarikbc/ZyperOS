# NES Player 2 on the handheld (src/p2.c, src/p2panel.c, src/main.c): the
# in-game menu's row on an NES game, the page it opens over the paused game
# (how a friend joins, on the handheld's own Wi-Fi or a computer on the USB
# cable, and who is playing), and the notices over the game as a player
# joins or leaves. The play page itself, on the friend's phone, is
# play.txt's. Its words for the network and the password are hotpanel.txt's
