The GKD Pixel 2 has a good screen, a speaker, a headphone jack and room for a big card, so it makes a handy music and video player too. ZyperOS has one built in, called MP4, for music, audiobooks and videos. SELECT opens it from anywhere, even from a paused game. This guide shows where files go, which ones play well, and the controls.

Where files go

WhatFolderExample
MusicMusic/, a folder per artist, one per albumMusic/Radiohead/The Bends/01 Planet Telex.mp3
AudiobooksAudiobooks/, a folder per book, an author's folder above it if you likeAudiobooks/Iain M. Banks/The Hydrogen Sonata/
VideosVideos/, a film on its own or a folder per seriesVideos/Planet Earth/01 From Pole to Pole.mp4

Send them as you send games: with the card in a computer, or on the File Transfer page over Wi-Fi or the USB cable. The adding games guide shows how.

  • An album folder put straight into Music/ moves under its artist when its tags name one. Songs in no folder at all move into Music/Singles/.
  • A book is one M4B file or a folder of MP3s, played in name order.
  • A picture named like a film, Where the Light Is.jpg, is its cover. Otherwise MP4 takes one from the video itself.

Formats that play

Music and books: MP3, M4A, M4B, MP4, AAC, FLAC, Ogg, Opus and WAV.

Video is where the Pixel 2 needs care. It has no hardware video decoder its Linux can use, so its four CPU cores decode in software:

VideoOn the Pixel 2
H.264 in an .mp4, up to 720pPlays smoothly
H.264 at 854x480 or 640x360The size to make for its 640x480 screen, at a fraction of the battery
HEVC and VP9Play up to about 480p
1080pOnly just keeps up, and drops frames when the picture moves a lot
AV1Does not play

MKV, WebM, MOV and AVI files play too, if the video inside them is one of those. For a long trip, convert films to H.264 at 854x480: they look as sharp on this screen and the battery lasts much longer.

The shelf and Now Playing

MP4 opens on a shelf of album covers, the way a console opens on its games. MENU > Show switches between music, books and videos. A on an album opens its tracks; A on a book carries on where you left it.

ButtonOn Now Playing
APause or play
L1 / R1The previous or next track, or a book's chapter
Left / RightBack or ahead ten seconds; held, a minute a step, then five
YThe play mode for music; the speed for a book, 0.75x to 2x
SELECTClose MP4; the music keeps playing

Leave MP4 and the music goes on behind the menus. On the home and a games shelf, L2 and R2 then skip tracks, and Zyper pops up with what plays now. While music plays, a running game is silent; pause the music and the game is heard again.

On a video, Y switches between full screen, where the controls show only when you press a button, and a view with the controls always on screen. Every video and every book remembers where you stopped, even through a power-off.

Lock the screen for a pocket

Press X on any MP4 screen while something plays. With music or a book the screen goes dark, and every button is ignored except the volume keys. On a book, that took about 6 hours of listening to 10. A video stays in sight. Hold SELECT and START together for two seconds to unlock.

Covers

An album's cover comes from its own files, or a picture in its folder. For albums with none, or only a small one, MENU > Album Art on MP4's shelf fetches a sharper cover over Wi-Fi from MusicBrainz's Cover Art Archive. It needs a USB Wi-Fi adapter on the Pixel 2.

Headphones

Wired headphones in the jack, or a USB-C headphone adapter in the port, take the sound the moment they are plugged in. The Pixel 2 has no Bluetooth, so Bluetooth headphones do not work on it.
